Who We Are

A healthcare knowledge portal engineered for everyday human clarity.

Himkiv is a digital healthcare platform that brings useful healthcare information and practical tools together in one place, helping users explore medicines, perform supported dose calculations, complete health surveys, and search for medicine information.

We do not act as an automated doctor, nor do we issue prescriptions. Instead, we structure clinical pharmacology data — from WHO ICD-11 MMS classifications and pharmacopoeia formularies — into an easy-to-read, transparent format that empowers patients, caregivers, and curious learners to have more informed conversations with their doctors.

The Problem We Address

Why Himkiv Exists

Finding trustworthy, comprehensible healthcare information shouldn't be an overwhelming ordeal. Yet every day, millions face significant barriers.

Dense & Intimidating Jargon

Medical leaflets and standard package inserts are often filled with clinical terms that confuse everyday patients who simply want to understand basic usage guidelines.

Scattered & Unreliable Sources

Health information online is frequently fragmented across unverified blogs, ad-heavy forums, and conflicting sites that create anxiety rather than clarity.

Difficult to Search

Most generic search engines lack typo-tolerance for drug spellings (like mistyping generic compounds) and fail to distinguish between commercial brand names and active generic salts.

The Himkiv Solution: Himkiv is designed to make this entire experience simpler. While we do not solve every healthcare challenge, we provide clean, calm tools that organize complex drug salts and symptoms into structured, accessible insights.

Safety & Transparency Commitment

Calm, clear guidelines on what Himkiv does and does not do

We believe true healthcare innovation begins with honesty regarding platform boundaries. Himkiv provides healthcare information and digital tools for educational guidance; it does not replace qualified medical practitioners.

Educational Reference: All data is structured from established pharmacopoeias (WHO EML, BNF) to assist patient literacy.
No Prescriptions: Medicine suggestions must never be treated as personal prescriptions or automated medical advice.
Independent Verification: Dose calculator outputs must always be independently validated before clinical administration.
Emergency Protocol: In an acute medical emergency, contact emergency ambulance (112 / 911) without delay.
