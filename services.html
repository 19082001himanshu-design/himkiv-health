Service 02 Posology Intelligence

Weight-Based Dose Calculator

Correct medication dosing depends critically on a patient's exact body weight, age category, and liquid formulation strength — particularly for infants and young children. Himkiv provides an interactive calculation tool calibrated against pharmacopoeia posology rules.

What It Does: Computes single-dose milligrams, daily mg limits, and oral suspension volume (mL) based on input weight (kg/lbs) and age.
How It Works: Implements BNF and WHO Model Formulary equations (e.g. Paracetamol 15 mg/kg QDS, Amoxicillin 40 mg/kg TDS) with hard safety ceiling caps.
Liquid Formulation Conversion: Translates pure milligram dosages into exact milliliter syringe markers for common pediatric drops and syrups (e.g. 120mg/5mL or 250mg/5mL).
Clinical Validation Mandatory: Dose calculator outputs are strictly informational reference aids. Always cross-verify calculated dosages with a licensed pediatrician or prescribing doctor before administration.

Service 03 Clinical Intake

Step 1 — Condition & Symptom Matcher: Filter through 130+ clinical conditions categorized by organ system with instant search.
